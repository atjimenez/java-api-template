28. COMPLIANCE STATUSES
==================================================

Use only:

PASS
PARTIAL PASS
FAIL
NOT APPLICABLE
NOT VERIFIED
AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE CONFLICT

Definitions:

PASS:
The requirement is fully satisfied and verified using repository or execution evidence.

PARTIAL PASS:
Some mandatory elements are satisfied, but one or more mandatory elements are incomplete or inconsistent.

FAIL:
A mandatory requirement is violated or missing.

NOT APPLICABLE:
The requirement genuinely does not apply, with a specific justification.

NOT VERIFIED:
Evidence is insufficient or required execution was not performed.

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE CONFLICT:
Two or more authoritative requirements conflict and prevent an objective decision.

Do not mark PASS when evidence is incomplete.

==================================================
29. SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

Use:

CRITICAL:
Immediate security, credential, data integrity, contractual, destructive-operation, or severe production risk.

HIGH:
Mandatory violation that can cause contract failure, unauthorized access, incorrect behavior, build failure, test enforcement failure, or major operational failure.

MEDIUM:
Mandatory violation causing significant maintainability, traceability, testing, integration, logging, configuration, or operational risk.

LOW:
Limited mandatory compliance issue with low immediate impact.

INFORMATIONAL:
Verified observation that does not violate a mandatory requirement.

Do not inflate severity.

- Contract-first and OpenAPI compliance: 15%
- Scope compliance: 10%
- Architecture and middleware compliance: 10%
- Security compliance: 15%
- API response, exception, and constants compliance: 10%
- Testing and WireMock compliance: 10%
- JaCoCo per-class compliance: 10%
- Postman compliance: 5%
- JMeter compliance: 5%
- Logging, correlation ID, configuration, and health compliance: 5%
- Build and verification compliance: 5%

Rules:

- NOT APPLICABLE requirements must be removed from the applicable denominator.
- NOT VERIFIED requirements must not be treated as PASS.
- Mandatory requirements have greater impact than informational observations.
- A Critical finding prevents an overall PASS.
- An unresolved High finding prevents an overall PASS.
- Contract-first FAIL prevents an overall PASS.
- Security FAIL prevents an overall PASS.
- Maven verification NOT VERIFIED prevents a verified production-readiness PASS.
- Per-class coverage NOT VERIFIED prevents JaCoCo compliance from being marked PASS.
- A score must not hide Critical or High findings.
